# DiskSizer landing page: industrial direction

The landing page now leads with “DISK FULL. OF WHAT?” and the everyday problem of forgotten recordings, archives, and exports. The visual direction uses condensed display type, warm paper, black panels, safety orange, hard edges, and storage-inspection labels.

The hero illustration is explicitly labeled as example files. The product section retains real screenshots and offers keyboard-accessible tabs for largest files, the disk map, and file previews. Numbered workflow rows explain how to inspect files before deciding what to recycle. Native disclosure controls hold the detailed accounting, saved-scan, platform, and unsigned-build information.

Fonts are served locally with their OFL licenses. Styles and scripts are separate versioned assets. The social preview and favicon match the new identity. Downloads still point to the verified v1.1.0 Windows ZIP; the unreleased performance work is not advertised as part of that download.

Validation: Chromium rendering at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 320 pixels; no horizontal page overflow; loaded images/fonts; screenshot tab selection and arrow-key navigation; full-size screenshot links; FAQ controls; anchor targets; unique IDs; download URLs; and structured-data version/price. Desktop and mobile renders were visually reviewed, including adjustments to narrow-screen heading and button wrapping.

The page contains no third-party runtime scripts, automatic animation, invented testimonials, or unsupported speed claims.
